## 1. Project Overview

This project brings together my Everyday white T-shirt storefront and my professional portfolio. The goal is to create two websites that are simple to navigate, visually consistent, and useful to their intended audiences.

The Everyday website focuses on presenting a basic white T-shirt as an everyday wardrobe essential. The portfolio website presents my projects, career interests, and professional development, with an emphasis on exploring AI-related careers.

My existing career research serves as the foundation for the portfolio. It compares AI Engineer and AI Forward Deployed Engineer roles, including their responsibilities, skills, and qualifications.

## 2. Two Possible Approaches

### Approach A: Simple Informational Websites

This approach would prioritize basic layouts, clear headings, readable text, and straightforward navigation. The Everyday website would mainly display product information, while the portfolio would organize projects and career research into separate sections.

**Advantages:**
- Easy to navigate.
- Less complicated to develop.
- Keeps the information organized.

**Disadvantages:**
- Could feel visually plain.
- Provides fewer opportunities to make the projects memorable.

### Approach B: Visual and Interactive Websites

This approach would use stronger visual layouts, product imagery, project previews, and interactive navigation. Everyday would emphasize the product and its branding, while the portfolio would make projects and career information easier to explore.

**Advantages:**
- Makes the websites more engaging.
- Gives each project a stronger visual identity.
- Helps visitors explore information more naturally.

**Disadvantages:**
- Requires more development and testing.
- Additional features could make navigation unnecessarily complicated.

### Final Decision

I will use a combination of both approaches. The websites should have visually appealing layouts while keeping navigation and information straightforward. This provides a balance between appearance, usability, and functionality.

The intended audience is people who prefer simple, understated clothing and want an uncomplicated everyday white T-shirt. The website should make it easy to understand the product and find important purchasing information.
