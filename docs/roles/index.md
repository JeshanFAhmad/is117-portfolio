# Emerging AI roles

AI teams use a growing mix of titles. The same title can mean different things at different companies, so compare the work and the team context as well as the name.

| Role | Core focus | Often works with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Forward Deployed Engineer](/roles/ai-forward-deployed-engineer) | Bring AI systems into a customer's real workflow | Customers, product, engineering |
| [AI Engineer](/roles/ai-engineer) | Build and operate software features powered by AI models | Product and platform teams |
| [AI Product Manager](/roles/ai-product-manager) | Decide which AI problems to solve and how to measure value | Users, design, data, engineering |
| [AI Safety & Evaluation](/roles/ai-safety-evaluation) | Measure model behavior, risks, and reliability | Research, policy, product, engineering |

These profiles are research frameworks, not universal job definitions. Use current postings to validate each responsibility and skill.
