# AI Safety & Evaluation roles

## What the role does

This family covers work that tests and improves how AI systems behave. Titles may include AI Evaluations Engineer, Model Behavior Analyst, AI Safety Researcher, Red Team Specialist, or Trustworthy AI Engineer. They are related but not interchangeable.

## Typical responsibilities to look for

- Define behavioral requirements and evaluation criteria
- Build test sets and repeatable evaluation pipelines
- Probe model failures, misuse risks, and edge cases
- Analyze results and communicate severity and uncertainty
- Partner with research and product teams to improve systems

## Common skill areas

Depending on the job: software or data analysis, experiment design, statistics, security testing, policy understanding, and careful technical writing. Some roles are research-heavy; others focus on evaluation tooling, operations, or governance.

## Portfolio idea

Create a small, clearly scoped benchmark for one model behavior. Document the test design, limitations, scoring process, and examples where the evaluation could mislead.
