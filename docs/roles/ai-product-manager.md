# AI Product Manager

## What the role does

An AI Product Manager guides the development of AI-powered products or features. The job connects user needs and business goals with the capabilities, limitations, and operating costs of AI systems.

## Typical responsibilities to look for

- Select and prioritize problems where AI may provide value
- Define product requirements and success measures
- Coordinate design, engineering, data, legal, and operations partners
- Plan user research, launch, feedback, and iteration
- Communicate uncertainty, risks, and trade-offs clearly

## Common skill areas

Product discovery, writing, prioritization, analytics, experimentation, AI literacy, and cross-functional communication. Technical depth expectations differ widely; inspect whether the posting asks for hands-on prototyping, ML experience, or mainly product leadership.

## Portfolio idea

Write a short product brief for an AI feature. Include user evidence, a baseline solution, evaluation metrics, failure handling, privacy questions, and a staged rollout plan.
