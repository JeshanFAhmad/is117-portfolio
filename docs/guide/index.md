# Getting started with AI career research

Start with work you want to do, then identify roles whose daily responsibilities match it. Job titles are noisy signals, especially in a fast-changing field.

1. Pick a problem area: building products, working with customers, research, infrastructure, data, or safety.
2. Collect 5–10 recent postings with different titles.
3. Compare responsibilities and required skills, not just the headline.
4. Build a small project that demonstrates one or two recurring skills.
5. Ask practitioners how the role works on their team, and update your notes.

[Use the job-posting worksheet](/guide/job-research) to structure your comparison.
